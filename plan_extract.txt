1. Background & Problem Context
The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) provides financial assistance to artisans,
weavers, and micro-entrepreneurs from marginalized communities. Physical melas (Shilp Samagam,
Surajkund Mela, Dilli Haat) create temporary sales spikes, but artisans lack a continuous, year-round
digital sales channel. This project builds that channel.
Critical Pain Points

Low Digital Literacy & Language Barrier — traditional e-commerce seller dashboards are
overwhelming and complex for this audience.

Photography & Presentation Gap — raw smartphone photos look unprofessional next to studio-shot
e-commerce products.

Cataloging & Description Barrier — difficulty writing SEO-friendly English/Hindi titles, descriptions,
and craft specifications.

Suboptimal Pricing — no visibility into market trends leads to underpricing or unsellable overpricing.
Core Solution
An intuitive, accessible, voice-first, mobile-responsive web application — the “Virtual Business
Manager” — that reduces product digitization to a simple three-step workflow: Capture → Speak → Sell.
2. System Architecture Overview
The application is organized into four functional pillars that sit on top of a shared backend and data layer.
Each pillar maps directly to one stage of the artisan's workflow, and together they feed a central admin
analytics layer for MoSJE.
Pillar
Purpose
1. AI Studio
Turns raw product photos into clean, professional e-commerce images.
2. Voice Cataloger
Converts spoken product descriptions (regional language) into structured,
SEO-ready listings.
3. Dynamic Pricing
Recommends a fair, market-aware price range from cost and labor inputs.
4. Smart Inventory &
Linkage
Publishes the finished listing to buyers via storefront, PDF/WhatsApp catalog,
B2B inquiries, and ONDC/GeM.
All four pillars read from and write to a shared data layer (artisan profiles, product listings, pricing history,
B2B inquiries), which the MoSJE Admin Dashboard aggregates for impact reporting.

3. Component-by-Component Breakdown
COMPONENT 1
AI Image Enhancer & Studio
Turns a messy smartphone photo into a clean, sellable product image — no photography skill required.

Interactive Camera/Upload — drag-and-drop or camera capture with on-screen framing guides.

AI Background Removal — client-side edge-detection and canvas masking to strip cluttered
backgrounds.

Studio Backdrop Presets — Clean White, Warm Marble, Wooden Surface, Fabric Texture, Soft
Shadow.

Auto Lighting & Sharpness Correction — canvas filters for contrast, brightness, saturation, and
drop-shadow generation.
COMPONENT 2
Multilingual Auto-Cataloger (Voice-to-Listing)
Lets an artisan simply speak about their product in their own language — the system writes the listing.

Voice Note Recorder — large mic button, records in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati,
Kannada, or Hinglish.

Speech Processing & NLP Engine — speech-to-text transcription with auto-translation into
professional English and Hindi.

Smart Catalog Generator — auto-fills SEO product title, craft type & material (e.g. Chanderi Silk,
Terracotta Pottery, Dhokra Craft, Wooden Carving), key features & dimensions, washing/care
instructions, and search tags/keywords.
COMPONENT 3
Dynamic ML Pricing Assistant
Stops artisans from underselling their work by giving a data-backed price range.

Cost Factor Inputs — raw material cost (■), labor hours spent, craft complexity rating.

AI Price Calculator — computes Minimum Base Cost (material + fair hourly wage), a Benchmark
E-Commerce Range (modelled market averages for similar craft categories), and a Recommended
Retail Price with a margin breakdown chart.
COMPONENT 4
Artisan Storefront & B2B Market Linkage
Turns a finished listing into something buyers — both retail and bulk — can actually find and act on.

Artisan Micro-Store — a digital storefront per artisan with a shareable link and QR code.

Instant PDF/WhatsApp Catalog Generator — one-click printable PDF catalog for buyers.

B2B Bulk Inquiry Portal — direct inquiry form for wholesale buyers, craft stores, and government
procurement officers.

ONDC / GeM Schema Exporter — exports products as JSON structured for the Open Network for
Digital Commerce and Government e-Marketplace.
